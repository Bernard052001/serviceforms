CONTRACT FOR WATER SERVICE INSTALLATION

I am register concessionaires of water service connection with Account Number ________________.

Contract is hereby make for the above water service installed on the date as stated to be used by me in accordance with the Rules and Regulations of the Tiaong Water District now enforced or may be enforced thereafter.

In addition I hereby agree:

1. That while I have in my custody the above-mention water meter, I shall at all times safeguard and protect it and the district has the right to inspect the water meter anytime.

2. That in case of damage of whatever cause, I shall report the same to the office of the Tiaong Water District within twenty four hours. I shall bear the cost of its repair except when due to force or other natural causes.

3. That in any case of loss or irreparable damages shall either replace the water meter in kind or pay for it at the current price in the market.

4. That I will abide by the terms and conditions stipulated in the contract for the Water Service and Installation of Water Meters.

5. That I understand that the Water Meters installed is properly sealed and in case of accidental damages on it shall notify the office immediately.

6. That I am fully aware of the penalties imposed by the law as provide in the Presidential Decree No. 198 (as amended by Presidential Decree No. 786) stated hereunder.


PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 198 (as amended by PD 786)

Section 1. Protection of Water and Facilities of District

(d) Safeguard and protect uses of its water, for this purpose, any person who installs any connection without the previous authority from the district established under this Decree; tampers water meters or uses jumpers or other devices stolen of pilfered water or water meters shall upon conviction be punished by prison correctional in the minimum period of fine of ranging from TWO THOUSAND PESOS or SIX THOUSAND PESOS or both. If the violation is committed with connivance or permission of an employee or officer of the water district and employee or officer shall, upon conviction, be punished by a penalty one degree lower than Prison Correctional in its minimum permanently be dismissed and perpetually disqualified from employment in any utility or service company owned or controlled by the government.
